# Money

Money / 财务 opens `/money` after Progress. It records expenses only; income,
accounts, debt, budgets, refunds, and charts are deferred. No currency conversion
or mixed-currency total is produced. Records do not change projects or supplies.

See [ui.md](ui.md), [data-model.md](data-model.md), and [web-implementation.md](web-implementation.md).
